# Unmute1AI Resilience Architecture v0.1

Initial public architecture disclosure for the Unmute1AI resilience stack.

This release documents the system-level relationship between:

- AnnealMesh — reasoning evaluation and verification
- PhaseFlow — continuous risk state
- Sentinel — governance and authorization
- RCM — persistent context and evidence
- IGNIS — governed recovery and repair
- Signal — accessible communication

The architecture treats reasoning integrity, persistent state,
containment, and recovery as parts of one AI-native system.

Unmute1AI is a member of the NVIDIA Inception program.

Recent NVIDIA work around AI-native storage, encryption,
integrity verification, compression, and recovery reinforces
the broader infrastructure direction we have independently
been developing.

co-development, or derivation of either party's work.
